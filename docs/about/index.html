About

About PrecisionCNC — CNC Machine Shop in Dongguan, China

Founded in 2011. 45 CNC machining centres across 4,000 m², 80 employees, of whom 22 are engineers and quality staff — the ratio that decides whether a machine shop solves problems or just runs parts.

Who we are

The awkward middle territory, on purpose

We machine for product development teams and hardware companies across Europe, North America and Asia. Not the biggest shop, and deliberately not the cheapest — the clients who stay are the ones who got tired of choosing between two bad options:

OptionThe problem
Local prototype shop Fast and communicative, but 3–5× the price once a part needs more than ten pieces.
Mass-production factory Cheap at volume, rigid about everything else. Prototypes become an afterthought and process continuity breaks between phases.

Our niche sits between them: prototypes that must match production intent, and low-volume production runs where consistency matters more than the last few percent of unit price.

How we differ

Four things we do differently from the average machining quote

None of these are marketing features. They are the reasons customers stop shopping around after the second order.

Engineers answer RFQs

Material and tolerance advice is included in the quotation, not upsold as a service. If your drawing is over-specified, the reply says so — with the price difference attached.

First-article CMM report, standard

Every new part number ships with CMM-verified first-article data. You see the measured numbers before committing to volume — not a certificate of conformance that says “within specification”.

No process drift between phases

The CAM program and fixture from your prototype run your production batch. No silent re-posting, no “production version” that mysteriously differs from the part you validated.

Honest lead times

We would rather lose an order than promise five days and ship in nine. If a date is at risk, you hear it from us before you would have found out yourself.

Working with us

What the first order looks like

  1. Send filesSTEP or IGES for geometry, plus a 2D PDF marking the tolerances that matter. Quantity and target date help more than you would expect.
  2. Quote in 24 hoursUnit price, price breaks, material and finish recommendations, and a delivery date. Any design feedback comes with the quote.
  3. Approval and productionMaterial is drawn against certificates; first article is machined and CMM-verified before the run continues.
  4. Inspection and shippingDimensional report, material certificate and finish certification travel with the parts. DDP or DAP, your choice.

On communication. Technical questions get answered by the engineer who reviewed your part, in writing, within one working day. No account manager relaying messages and no “let me check with production” for three days.

On NDAs and IP. We sign customer NDAs before files are released. We do not keep customer designs beyond the retention period your contract specifies, and we do not manufacture customer parts for anyone else. Ask for our mutual template if you need one.
