Common questions

Before you ask

What is your minimum order quantity?

One piece. Prototypes from a single part are how most of our customer relationships start — it is the cheapest place to test a machine shop.

How fast can I get a quote?

Within 24 hours of receiving a STEP file and a 2D drawing. The reply includes unit pricing, price breaks, material and finish recommendations, and a delivery date we intend to keep.

Drawings only, or do you need 3D CAD?

We need 3D geometry to quote accurately. STEP or IGES is ideal — export STEP from whatever CAD package you use rather than sending native files.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Send yours and we return a signed copy before you release files, or ask us for a mutual NDA template.

Start with one part

Test us where it is cheapest to test — a prototype. Upload a STEP file and a marked-up drawing; you will have engineering feedback and a price within 24 hours.
