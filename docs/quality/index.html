Quality

Quality Control & Inspection

ISO 9001:2015 certification is the entry ticket, not the differentiator. What matters is what happens at the machine and at the CMM — here is our actual workflow.

Incoming

Material certificates verified against purchase orders; PMI spot checks on stainless and titanium; hardness checks on heat-treated stock before it reaches a machine.

In-process

First-article inspection on every setup — the operator cannot run production until FAI passes. Probing on mill-turn work; SPC and Cpk reporting above 500 pieces.

Final

100% inspection on critical features, AQL sampling on general dimensions. Zeiss CMM for geometric tolerances, optical comparator for profile, roughness tester for finish.

Documentation

Dimensional report standard with every order. Full FAI package (AS9102-style), Cpk data and heat-lot traceability on request.

Inspection equipment

What we measure with

Tolerances are only meaningful relative to the equipment that verifies them. A ±0.005 mm claim verified on a caliper is a marketing number, not a measurement.

EquipmentUsed forPractical resolution
Zeiss CMM Geometric tolerances — flatness, true position, perpendicularity, profile; first articles on all new part numbers Sub-micron measurement uncertainty in a temperature-controlled room
Optical comparator Profile features, thread form, small radii and chamfers that are hard to touch-probe 0.001 mm with a good edge
Height gauge & gauge blocks Locating heights, datum offsets, quick verification during a run 0.001 mm
Thread gauges Go / no-go verification of metric, UNC/UNF, NPT and BSPP threads Class-of-fit verification
Surface roughness tester Ra verification on sealing faces, bearing bores and cosmetic surfaces Ra 0.01 µm
Optical & laser scanning Free-form surfaces and full-part deviation maps on request Colour deviation map against the nominal CAD
Documentation

What ships with your parts

Standard with every order:

  • Dimensional inspection report against the drawing
  • Material certificate per EN 10204 3.1 (metal orders)
  • Finish certification where a finish was specified
  • Packing list with quantity and part number

Available on request:

  • Full FAI package, AS9102-style
  • Cpk / SPC data on production characteristics
  • Heat-lot traceability and PMI reports
  • Inspection data in your format (CSV, Excel, Q-DAS)
  • Certificate of conformance with authorised signature
Non-conformance

When something goes wrong

Real talk. With 45 machines running mixed prototypes, things occasionally go wrong. Our non-conformance process is why customers stay.

  1. You hear it from us firstBefore you find it. If we catch a problem in final inspection, the email goes out the same day — not after you chase a late shipment.
  2. Disposition within 24 hoursRework, replacement or credit, decided and communicated in one working day.
  3. Documented root causeWritten cause analysis with the corrective action, sent with the replacement parts.
  4. Updated reportsNew first-article data on whatever comes out of the fix — you do not have to ask for it.

What we do not do: ship a non-conforming batch and hope it passes incoming inspection downstream. It costs us an order, but it costs you a production line.
