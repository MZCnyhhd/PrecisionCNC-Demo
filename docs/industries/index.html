Industries

Industries We Serve

Six sectors account for almost all of our work. Each one has its own documentation expectations, material habits and failure modes — and we have been through the audit conversations before.

01

Medical Devices

Prototypes and low-volume production for surgical instruments, trial components and device enclosures. We work in 316L and PEEK daily, with passivation and clean packaging available on request.

For design-control audits we provide documented sub-tier process records — material certificates, inspection reports, process parameters and revision traceability.

An honest boundary. We do not claim ISO 13485. We support ISO 13485-certified customers as a documented sub-tier supplier, and we will tell you plainly which parts of your quality system we can and cannot satisfy. A supplier who overstates their certification is a problem you discover during an audit.

  • 316L stainless and PEEK, plus titanium for instruments
  • Passivation per ASTM A967 with certification
  • Electropolishing coordinated for surgical finish
  • Full dimensional report with every order; FAI on request

02

Aerospace & UAV

5-axis structural brackets, flight test hardware and UAV frames in 7075 and titanium, with FAI documentation, material certificates and tooling control.

The advantage for development programmes is prototype-to-production continuity: the same CAM program and the same fixture that made your three prototypes makes your three hundred production parts. No process drift between phases, no re-qualification.

  • Single-setup 5-axis machining keeps inter-feature position within ±0.01 mm
  • AS9102-style first article inspection packages
  • Heat-lot traceable material with EN 10204 3.1 certificates
  • Tooling retained and controlled so re-runs match the original

Weight is a design decision, not a machining one. If we can remove 30 grams from a bracket by changing a fillet radius or a pocket depth, the drawing review will say so — before the parts are cut, not after.

03

Robotics & Automation

Actuator housings, harmonic drive components and custom end-of-arm tooling. In this sector, repeatability across batches is the product: batch #200 has to match batch #2, dimensionally and cosmetically.

That means retention tooling, documented process parameters, and anodise colour batches managed per order rather than dumped into a common tank.

  • Harmonic drive and cycloidal component machining to tight position tolerance
  • Mill-turn for actuator shafts with cross-ports and flats in one setup
  • End-of-arm tooling in 6061 and 7075, bead-blast and anodise finish
  • Repeat orders run against the original CAM program and fixture

Repeatability by numbers

Process control
SPC charts and Cpk reporting on runs above 500 pieces
Fixture control
Tooling numbered, retained and re-used; revisions documented
Anodise colour
Batches tracked per order; samples approved before the run
CAM continuity
Prototype program is the production program — no silent re-post
Traceability
Part number, revision, heat lot and inspection record archived
04

Automotive & EV

Test rigs, prototype components and charging infrastructure parts. Low-volume work with automotive-grade documentation: PPAP-style dimensional reports on request, Cpk data on critical characteristics, and revision control that survives your change process.

  • Powertrain and battery-pack test fixtures
  • EV charging connector and housing components
  • Bracket and mounting hardware in 6061, 6082 and 7075
  • Inspection data supplied in your format (CSV, Excel, Q-DAS)
05

Semiconductor Fixtures

Vacuum-compatible aluminium and stainless fixtures, PEEK insulators and clean-finish requirements handled at the finishing stage — targeted bead blasting, dedicated tooling for PEEK, and packaging that keeps the finish intact in transit.

  • Vacuum-compatible materials, low outgassing finishes
  • PEEK and PTFE insulators, wafer handling components
  • Flatness-controlled plate work with CMM mapping
  • Clean packaging, gloves-on handling, no bare-metal contact
06

Consumer Electronics

Appearance parts in anodised aluminium — bead-blast and anodise cosmetic finishing with colour consistency managed per batch. The difference between a good appearance part and a rejected one is usually finishing discipline, not machining.

  • 6061-T6 bezels, buttons, heat sinks and enclosures
  • Bead-blast + Type II anodise with colour matching
  • Laser engraving for logos and serial numbers
  • First-article cosmetic samples approved before the run
